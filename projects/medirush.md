# MediRush — AI + IoT Safety & Health Venture

## Overview
MediRush is a student venture exploring wearable and IoT systems for safety and health monitoring, including industrial-worker risk detection and remote monitoring scenarios.

My involvement has included co-founding / technical contribution across the product concept, AI/IoT architecture, wearable sensing ideas, and competition delivery.

## Product direction
The project has explored:
- wearable vital-sign monitoring;
- location / geofencing;
- fall and incident detection;
- restricted-area / proximity alerts;
- emergency escalation;
- worker + supervisor notifications;
- ESP32-based communication and sensing concepts.

## Engineering lesson
MediRush exposed me to the gap between an engineering prototype and a deployable product: sensing reliability, communications, battery/power constraints, user safety, false alarms, hardware/software integration, and the need to validate product claims with evidence.
